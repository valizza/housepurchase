How each price breaks down
Down payment 25% + Buyer's Stamp Duty is the upfront both of you fund. The 5% minimum-cash slice must be cash — CPF can't cover it. Adjust the split above to change how the upfront and the monthly loan divide between the two of you.

Financing — repaid monthly to the bank

The 75% you don't pay upfront is the bank's loan — separate from the upfront cost above. It disburses progressively: the first 5% is drawn at foundation stage, which is when monthly repayments begin, and the whole loan is paid back monthly with interest over the tenure.

Note
The 5% foundation drawdown isn't an extra cost — it's the first slice of the 75% loan. Instalments start small (interest only on what's drawn so far) and rise as construction draws down more.

Notes. BSD uses the IRAS residential tiers in force since Feb 2023 (1% / 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% / 6%). The model assumes a first bank loan at the 75% LTV cap, so 25% down with a 5% cash minimum, and ABSD at 0% (first property). The 5% must be cash — CPF can't cover it; the remaining 20% down and the stamp duty can come from CPF OA (on a new launch, BSD is often paid in cash first and reimbursed from CPF). Upfront and monthly figures divide by the split you set above. This is a planning tool, not financial advice — confirm final figures with your banker and conveyancer.
